## 3 · Windows light up when a card appears ❌
**What you'd see.** Open Today: the front card's window starts dark and lights pane by pane up to where you really are, the gold arc on the
frame grows with it. Swipe to another card: *that* window lights as it arrives. The old Today did this; the new carousel had lost it.
**Exact behaviour on the website** (`src/app/heir/ui/today.tsx`, `CardWindow`):
- Applies to **every card with a window you have progress on**: recommended, finished, extra, and "Done for today".
- Starts **dark** (all panes unlit, arc at 0), waits until the card is **at least 60% on screen** and the glass has loaded, then after **350 ms**
  lights up to the real progress (never beyond it).
- The sweep lasts about `min(2400, litPanes × 130) + 600` ms, and the gold arc grows over the same time.
- Once per appearance (leaving and coming back to Today plays it again).
- When it has settled, a lesson card's **next pane glows softly** (the "hint"); dust (needs refresh) is applied only after settling.
- A course not started yet keeps the dim moving light behind dark glass, as before. Reduce-motion users get the final state with no animation.
**In the app:** `features/today/today_cards.dart` builds `RoundWindow(... lit: window.lit ...)` directly, so the window is simply drawn at its
final state. Wrap it so it starts dark and animates when visible. `RevealWindow` (the lesson-done window) already does something similar and
can be reused.

## 4 · One spreading patch, not scattered panes ❌
**What the owner saw.** On First Verses, finishing a lesson lit "a bunch of random panes" and it "took forever".
**Why.** Two causes. (a) The lesson-done window waited 150 ms per pane, and a verse is worth ~120 panes: about 18 seconds. (b) Each question owns
a share of the window's panes, but `pieces.json` lists them in id order, which jumps all over the glass; lighting a prefix of that list looks
like scattered panes.
**The fix (website).** `sweepOrder(ids, centres)` in `src/app/heir/ui/glass/light-motion.ts`, used by the full window and the small ones so
the same progress lights the same panes everywhere:
1. Take one question's panes (`flat.slice(floor(i·P/n), floor((i+1)·P/n))`, as before).
2. Start with the pane that comes first **clockwise from 12 o'clock** around the window centre (0.5, 0.5), after the widest angular gap, so a group
   straddling the top starts at its true start. Angle = `(atan2(y−0.5, x−0.5) + π/2 + 2π) mod 2π`, with `centres[id−1]` from `pieces.json`.
3. Then always step to the **nearest not-yet-chosen pane** (squared distance; ties to the lower id).
4. A fraction f lights the first `round(f × count)` panes of that order (so a half-learned verse is one patch).
Result on the real windows: jumps over 0.15 of the window dropped from about **27% of steps to about 1.5%**.
**Timing.** `revealTo` uses a stagger of `clamp(2400 / newPanes, 6, 130)` ms per pane, so any sweep takes about 2.5 s; a few new panes still get
a moment each. The lesson-done gold arc moves after `700 + min(2400, newPanes × 130) + 400` ms.
**In the app:** `features/window/light.dart` (`paneStates`) slices `flat` directly, and `reveal_window.dart` lights in fixed 280 ms stages. Port
`sweepOrder` into the one place panes are chosen (so full, small and reveal windows all agree), pass it the `centres` from `pieces.json`, and check the
lesson-done sweep takes about 2.5 s. **Add a test** that the same lit fractions give the same panes on the website and the app (the engine fixtures
approach in `docs/app/adr/0003` works: export `sweepOrder` outputs for a few windows and compare).
